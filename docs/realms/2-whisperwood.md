# Whisperwood (realm 2)

Realm 2 (`forest`), the prototype's second realm, reached on foot along the thorn road from Blackpine: its places, people, secrets, light, water, life and music.

- **The Warden's Stone**: where the thorn road comes in over the brook from Blackpine.
- **Hollowbough**: a village of great home trees round a lake, the Heartpool (a broad water with
  an arm reaching north-east and a bay to the south-west): doors and lit windows in the trunks,
  treehouses up in the crowns, lanterns strung between the trees, and the Heart Oak at the back of
  a broad island, a green in front of it round the gathering fire, reached by two rope bridges.
  Alder the Reeve, in the Heart Oak, knows where the Thorn Warden holds out; Moss the innkeeper
  sells Moon Flasks; Bryony the thorn-smith sharpens swords and, past realm 1's smith, tempers
  them twice more (levels 4 and 5, +25% each); Ash's sister Wren is missing; the old owl on the
  snag where the road comes in gives a hint each time you ask. The village goes about its day:
  a fisher on the jetty, washing at the bay, children chasing round the fire, the old man by it,
  a gardener, a carrier on the lakeside path, the watch at the east bridge, the beekeeper at her
  hives by the Whisper, the weaver at her door (she wraps boots in spider silk: quicker on your
  feet). 35 people live here, 32 of them out in the village (33 once Wren is home), most of them
  doing something: Clover on a rope swing on the north shore while Nutkin waits his turn;
  Granny Yarrow on the west shore telling Acorn, Teasel and Woad the Warden's tale (he was a
  keeper of the wood once, until something whispered to him under the Great Tree), a line at a
  time in bubbles to anyone near, while Midge chases moths; Kestrel of the Watch up in the elder's
  treehouse; Old Elm carving by the road east; the loom at Old Sorrel's door; Fern, Hob and Sloe
  coming home from the wood down the lanes; Linden the Lamplighter round the lakeside lanterns;
  Rush with her buckets. The village follows the story: four trees round it, scarred by the
  Warden's thorns, go green once the Thorn Heart is torn out; once the Warden falls, 13 lantern
  posts up the lanes out of the village light, one lane after another, and garlands go up between
  them.
- **The Owl and Acorn**: the inn tree where the road comes in has a room in its foot that you
  walk into: Moss behind her counter (she sells the Moon Flasks glowing on the shelf), a hearth,
  Robin playing the lute by the fire, Old Thistle and Burl at a round table; its voices and the
  lute carry out of the doorway. The Old Grove's first oak, between the camera and the room,
  fades like a crown when it would hide you.
- **The Gatherers' Clearing**, past the Whisper on the Blackwater's shore: Wren in a goblin cage.
- **The Ring of Oaks**, west of the village: a three-wave trial for the Heartwood Seed (one
  more heart) and 100 coins. Old Nettle lives in the glade south of it (her nettle tonic fills the
  blue bar faster).
- **Moon Shards**: three more, among the fen's pools, up where the vines climb, and on a giant's
  shelf in the High Canopy that only the rope walk over the Mirror Pool reaches (another heart).
  A cracked rock under the Overhang hides a niche.
- **Rookfall**: a gorge the Whisper falls into, winding north under the East Woods to where the
  river goes under the rock. A rope bridge crosses it; a fall in costs a heart.
- **The Warden's Hold**, up the rock stair between two hills by the Overhang: living thorns grow
  across the top of the stair, the Thorn Heart pulsing in them; tear it out and they wither. A
  short path leads to the Great Tree; clear the Warden's garrison before the hollow at its feet,
  and the thorns across its mouth draw back. The Thorn Warden waits in the hollow (room to move:
  16 by 15 m): it keeps its distance and shoots, and every attack shows first: the volley's lines
  lie on the ground and fix before the arrows fly along them (step aside or raise your shield),
  arrows rain on spots that glow and fill up before they land (step off them), it calls in
  goblins, and when enraged makes roots burst under you (their spots fill up too). One attack at a
  time. The fight is on foot: the stag waits outside.
- **The Stag's Thicket**: a hollow among mossy rocks in the Deep Wood, where the Warden's keepers
  guard the bound stag. North of it a cleft runs into the western heights, choked with living
  thorns a sword only scratches; the freed stag's thorn burst tears them away, and the stag's old
  bed lies beyond.
- **Off the paths**: the Deer Meadow's hunter's stand, the Whisper's Fall into Rookfall, a rock
  pillar in the gorge a running jump from the rim, the Rookery in the pines north of it (the
  rooks' hoard), the Bat Roost in the north cliff, the Fallen Giant lying across the Whisper, the
  Drowned Shrine out on the Blackwater's stepping stones, a fallen knight's cairn in the Withered
  Wood and the Warden's Seat behind the Great Tree (both on the Warden's heights), the Mushroom
  Dell, the kingfisher's bank of the east river, and a goblin camp by the brook. Each hides a
  chest, as do the Charcoal Kilns down a lane south of the village (goblins have taken them).
- **Its trees and colours**: each part of the wood has its own leaves: silver birches on the
  thorn road's verge, copper and gold beeches and the five ancient oaks in the Old Grove, blue-black
  pines in the East Woods and on the rims, lime-green oaks under dark pines in the Deep Wood, teal
  giants under the High Canopy, willow-greys by the waters, rust on the Warden's heights, each home
  tree its own green, and one tree in twelve an odd one (scarlet, gold, plum, silver, a gold larch,
  a blue spruce). White hawthorns blossom by the Heartpool, rowans hung with red berries line the
  lanes, a copper beech stands at the far side of three glades, and the Withered Wood's last
  beeches hold rust leaves. Pink campion and foxgloves grow in drifts, and ivy hangs down the
  cliffs (short and flowering, never to the foot: only the full curtains of vines can be climbed).
- **Its light**: a green-teal night, each place its own shade, blended where places meet. The
  mist lies thin and low over Hollowbough, the meadows and glades and the water, and thick only in
  the Deep Wood, the Mossfen (a sallow green fen) and on Rookfall's floor (a cold blue-green
  gorge). The Old Grove is old gold under its oaks; the Withered Wood, the Overhang and the thorn
  ravine carry the Warden's sickness, a violet-green, as do his hold and the Great Tree's roots;
  the Bat Roost, the Niche and the Owl and Acorn are lamplit, without mist. At dawn the light comes
  gold through the trunks, the shadows blue-teal, and the mist burns off.
- **Its water**: the Whisper runs east to its fall into Rookfall, the stream down Rookfall's floor
  runs fastest, the brook runs east into the Greywater and the Greywater south along the east
  edge: ripples and foam drift downstream, with white water at the Whisper's fall lip and round
  bridges over running water. The Heartpool, the Blackwater and the Mirror Pool lie still, the
  moon's path across them. Low banks slope into the water; shallows are clear over stones and
  weed, deeps dark and mirroring the sky; reeds grow in the still shallows and stones stand in
  the running ones.
- **Its life** (none of it fights): rooks wheel over the Rookery and Rookfall, walk the ground
  and go up together; long-eared bats come out of the Roost over the East Woods; wood ducks
  paddle on the Heartpool and the Blackwater; a night heron hunches at their edges; golden carp
  bask with their backs out and brown trout hold against the Whisper's and the brook's current,
  rising in rings; tree frogs sit on pink-flowered lily pads; a herd of red deer with its stag
  grazes the Deer Meadow and bolts together; rabbits feed at six warrens by the lanes; a badger
  comes out at the Old Grove's edge; and now and then the white hart of the old wood, its antlers
  gold-green, stands in a glade and bolts. Gold fireflies dance in every glade beside the wood's
  cyan lights, luna moths at the lanterns and over the meadows.
- **Its own music**: Whisperwood plays its own versions of the moods (a flute over harp and cello in
  D Dorian, soft hand drums), and its nights have birdsong. The Owl and Acorn has its own tune:
  Robin's lute, a jig in D Dorian over harp and cello, a hand drum on the table.
