# Realm scores

How much each built realm shares with the ones before it, area by area, scored as [common-and-unique.md](common-and-unique.md) explains, with the other measures behind each score.

## Realm 2 against realm 1 (2026-10-01)

| Area | Common | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| Quest steps | ~80% | The "where to go" part, so high is fine. But the captive and trial text was reused almost word for word, and a Pip lives in both villages |
| Rewards: chests, shards, the trial, moonfires | ~85% | Fine, the same reason |
| Village services | ~60% | Fine: the same services in a new kind of village |
| Stronghold and tyrant | ~60% | Fine: the same steps (lever, garrison, arena), a new fight |
| Map and route | ~75% | Too same: the same size, the same direction (south-east to north-west), the same counts |
| Kinds of place | ~40% | Near the aim; the marsh, the pine wood and the goblin camp are near twins |
| Terrain and look | ~55% | Near: new shapes and colour on the same trees and ground tiles |
| Mechanics | ~55% | On the aim: the knight's moveset is shared (as it should be), the realm's own rules are new |
| Foes | ~75% | Off: two thirds of what you fight are realm-1 foes in new colours |
| Music and sound | ~85% | Off: not one track of its own |
| **All** | **~67%** | **Aim: ~40%** |

Other measures of the same thing:
- 36 of realm 2's 54 placed foes are realm-1 types (67%); 46 stand on realm-1 bodies (85%). Only the
  spitter and the Warden run new AI.
- The ground-type mix overlaps 57%; realm 2 has no ground type of its own. 15 of its 29 regions have a
  realm-1 counterpart.
- In screenshots about 45% of the picture looks familiar; the colour of the light does most of the
  separating (realm 1's shots sit at hue 209-253°, realm 2's at 150-168°).
- Realm 2 plays 8 of realm 1's 9 music tracks and adds none; 69 of its 73 sound effects are realm 1's.
- The user's feedback on realm 2 never said it was too similar: every complaint asked for realm 1's
  quality (real zones, a purpose everywhere, nothing man-made in the wild). The land is where that work
  went, and it is the most original part. Foes and music got no such pass.

The follow-up ([its plan](../../board/plans/follow-up.md), groups 20 to 27) went after the excess in realm 2 itself; the map's frame (its
size, direction and counts) was kept on purpose and is a note for later realms.

Of 57 placed foes, 2 are realm 1's as they were (the Bat Roost's bats), 43 the same kinds in a new form, 12 new |

| Kinds of place | ~40% | ~38% | The stag's bed (a dell in the heights) |
| **All** | **~67%** | **~58%** | Aim ~40%: what's left is the shared frame and structure, and the shared ground tiles and base trees |

## Realm 3 against realms 1 and 2 (2026-10-02)

The same scoring: a part counts as common if a player who finished realm 1 or realm 2 would call it the
same thing. Measured twice: after the content round (about 48%), and after group 36's review and balance,
which dressed the crew in their own gear, gave the realm its own night and a first screen of sea, put the
shared spine back (the village and its leader first, a garrison before the tyrant's door) and gave the inn
and the mini-bosses music of their own. (Its other changes were numbers: health, timings, coins.) Words
are scored with the quest steps.

Other measures of the same thing:
- Of its 62 placed foes (the Tidelord apart), none is a realm-1 kind only recoloured any more: 46 are the
  same roles in a new form (21 of the crew: 11 goblins, 5 shield goblins, 4 archers and a brute, realm 1's
  kinds in the crew's own gear, src/game/models.ts; 13 divers: goblins in dive gear; 4 harpooners: the
  crew's ranged, with a line and a reel; 7 crabs: the shield goblin's guarded front on a sea creature;
  Brassbelly: a brute's blows and a steam vent) and 16 new (6 jellies, 5 eels, 4 pufferfish, Old Inkarm).
  Those 21 were realm 1's kinds in teal until group 36. The trial's 11 (a conch shaman among them) and the
  Tidelord's calls are realm 1's kinds in the crew's gear too. 7 of its 13 kinds run new AI, and Brassbelly
  adds a move to the brute's (realm 2: 2, the spitter and the Warden).
- The ground-type mix overlaps realm 1's by 12% and realm 2's by 10% (13% and 12% before group 36 reshaped
  the stair's foot and the isle's shores; realms 1 and 2 overlap 57%). A third of its ground is its own
  three types; half is sand. Its props are mostly its own (src/world/sea.ts); of the shared ones only rocks,
  barrels, crates, rowboats and a few bushes.
- In screenshots the sea, the sand and the stilt houses separate it, and now its light does too: realm 3's
  shots sit at hue 172-196° (eleven places from the stair's head to the palace), between realm 2's
  (150-168°) and realm 1's (205-253°); shots of both taken on the same build fall inside their old ranges.
  The same eleven places under its old night measure 194-215° (the content round's shots: 199-206°): its
  night alone moved them 14-22° toward green. Its night is its own (src/game/realms.ts: `SEA_NIGHT`'s
  sky light, mist and sea turned toward turquoise, against `CASTLE_NIGHT`'s blue and `FOREST_NIGHT`'s
  green-teal; src/world/terrain.ts: the moon's glints on its water sea-glass), and its first screen, at the
  Sea Stair's foot, looks over Stairfoot Cove's water. It comes nearest Whisperwood's on the strand, where
  pale sand fills the screen (172° against 168°).
- Water on 67% of the map (realm 1: 7%, realm 2: 18%), deep water on 53%.
- Counts, realms 1, 2, 3: named places 21, 31, 31 (Stairfoot Cove added); chests 11, 23, 33 (35 with the
  two that come up later, the bottle's and Old Inkarm's); people 7, 17, 34; lore stones 5, 9, 13; quests 7,
  5, 11; moonfires 4, 4, 3; signs 3, 3, 0.

Common to all three: the knight and his moveset, the status effects (no burning here), moonfire rest,
3 Moon Shards for a heart, chests and lore, a cracked wall, a captive in a cage, a relic trial of 3 waves
(3/4/4 foes), a village the main quest goes to first, with a leader who sends the knight on, an innkeeper,
a smith and a hint-giver, wares, a stronghold opened by a lever (a winch, a thorn heart, a bell), a
garrison whose fall opens the way to the tyrant, a tyrant's hall whose door shuts (two summon points,
enrage at half health, dawn), cliffs along the far edges, a way in that the last realm's beast opens, the
camera and pixel look.
