# For later realms (notes, not a plan)

Lessons from realms 2 and 3 for the realms still to come: what to decide on purpose, what worked, what to watch.


Written after realm 2, and what realm 3 did with each:
- The common part was "whatever realm 1 had", copied whole: the same map size and direction, the same
  counts of moonfires, shards, cages and waves, even the same lines. Decide the common 40% on purpose
  (likely the quest's shape, the village's services, rest and rewards) and let the rest change. *Realm 3's
  plan named it, and its frame (size, direction, counts) is its own.*
- A realm's foes should be mostly its own: most of what the player fights, not one creature in a crowd of
  recoloured goblins. *Under the sea they are, and since group 36 the crew wear their own gear: a third of its
  placed foes are realm 1's kinds and AI, but none only recoloured.*
- Each realm should sound like itself. The prototype had a track and an ambience per realm (`o40.js`
  TRACKS, `r22.js` THEMES_AMB). *Realm 3 had its own music and ambience from its first group.*
- Each border opens with the beast freed in the realm before; give that beast something to open. *The
  Thornstag's second leap clears the Sea Stair's rockfall; the serpent will swim the Dune Strait.*
- Difficulty: revisit hit damage, the sword list, flasks and what coins buy before realm 3. *The sword
  list and the wares grew and flasks heal more; hit damage is unchanged (realm 4's draft changes it).*

What worked in realm 3 (about 48% common on its first build, about 46% after its review and balance; realm 2
was 67%, 58% after a follow-up):
- Planning the 40/60 from the start, and building the realm's own ground, props, foes and music in its
  first groups instead of fixing them afterwards.
- A frame of its own: another size, the journey running the other way and downward, other counts.
- One rule at the realm's heart (water: the suit, air, floating, currents, bubble columns) that changes how
  the shared moveset plays without changing the moveset.
- The realm's tool won from a mini-boss (Brassbelly's suit): a step of the quest that is the realm's own.
- Its fights built to the fair-fight rules from the start: lines and rings that fill before the blow, one
  attack at a time, air a nuisance and never a killer.
- Measuring it straight after the content round, so that its review could fix what the measure found (the
  crew's gear, its own night and first screen, the shared spine) before the realm was called done.

What to watch:
- **Old kinds under a new dress.** The crew on the strand, the isles and before the palace (21 of 62
  placed foes), all of the trial's 11 and the Tidelord's calls are realm 1's goblins, shield goblins,
  archers, a brute and a shaman; group 36 dressed them as the realm's own, as group 24 dressed the Old
  Wood's (foes from about 54% to about 37%), but their AI, blows and wind-ups are still realm 1's. Kinds of
  a realm's own count for more than a new dress: realm 4's draft plan keeps one familiar face (goblin
  tomb-robbers in their own gear) and makes the rest its own.
- **The light, fixed late.** Realm 3's night was the Keep's blue (screens at hue 199-206°) and its first
  screens (down the Sea Stair: grass terraces, the sea out of view) read as the Keep's, until group 36 gave
  it a turquoise night (172-196°) and a cove at the stair's foot. Set a realm's light and its first screen
  in its first group: realm 4's draft does (an endless sunset, screens at hue 20-45°).
- **The shared spine, put back.** The content round's main quest pointed straight at the lighthouse isle,
  past the village and its leader, and the crew before the palace opened nothing; group 36 restored both
  (the village and Gannet first; the landing's crew hold the floodgate's winch). "Village, leader,
  garrison" is part of the common 40%, and realm 4's draft keeps it.
- **Volume grows with each realm**: chests 11, 23, 33; people 7, 17, 34; quests 7, 5, 11. Realm 3's
  content round paid about twice what its village sells until group 36 (now 20% over). Set the counts and
  the purse before a content round, and check them after it: realm 4's draft sets them first (24 chests,
  about 22 people, 8 quests, about 15% over).
- **Upgrades fade.** Each sword level adds less of what the knight has (+10% at level 7) and the list ends
  at 7; hearts reach 9. Realm 4 needs another kind of upgrade, or hits that cost more (its draft: a curved
  edge for reach, and a boss's blow costing two hearts).
- **Ranged foes nearly gone** (13%, from 36-41%): under the sea the threats come up close. Keep the mix
  varied: it's what made realm 2 harder in kind (realm 4's draft: about 28%).
