# 090 Realm 1's errands and finds

Group 90 of [the plan for realms 1 and 2](../plans/realms-1-2-revisit.md) (task [081](../in-progress/081-realms-1-2-as-beautiful-as-realm-3.md)).

## What

Five errands that change a place you can see: the miller's wheel jammed with goblin junk (cleared, it turns); the lamplighter's stolen oil (found, the road's lamps light up to the keep); Pip's cat in the Hollow; the shepherd's strays in Blackpine; the chapel's bell rope taken to Gnasher's camp (returned, the bell rings the hour). Lore 5 to ~12 (the barrows, the arch, the crypt, the Overlook, the mill, the island shrine, the eighth stone), chests 11 to ~18 where only the curious go. Things that answer the sword: bells, crows bursting up, falling apples, the well's bucket. The economy re-balanced to the same surplus.
