# 095 Whisperwood's errands, finds and sound

Group 95 of [the plan for realms 1 and 2](../plans/realms-1-2-revisit.md) (task [081](../in-progress/081-realms-1-2-as-beautiful-as-realm-3.md)).

## What

Four errands of the wood: the beekeeper's lost swarm, the forester's snares, Old Nettle's night-blooming flower in the Mossfen, a kite caught in the High Canopy. Lore 9 to ~13, chests 23 to ~28; birds' nests and fairy rings with small finds. Beds of its own: the canopy's hush and creak, the roar at the falls, a frog chorus, the village's chimes, voices and lute, cracking branches in the Deep Wood, a dawn chorus, a nightingale by the Heartpool. The economy re-balanced.
