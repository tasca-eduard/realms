# 081 Realms 1 and 2 made as beautiful and interesting as realm 3

realm 1 and 2, because realm 3 is very beutiful in comaprison - maybe we can make them also this much
better/interesting/beutiful". Realm 4 waits ([066](../blocked/066-read-the-realm-4-plan.md)).

## First: look again, side by side

Shots of realms 1 and 2 against realm 3 from the game camera (the same places' kinds: arrival, village, the way,
the wild, the stronghold, the tyrant's hall; night and dawn), and what realm 3 has that they lack, measured where
it can be: its own light and palette, water that reads, life everywhere (fish, birds, crabs, seals, boats, folk
about their day), set pieces (the lighthouse, the drowned kingdom, the wreck), places with a story, density of
things to find, sound. Then a plan (board/plans/realms-1-2-revisit.md) with groups, each with its checks, for the
user to read before building, as realm 3's plan was.

## Rules that hold

The design rules ([docs/design/design-rules.md](../../docs/design/design-rules.md)): each realm keeps its own
character (realm 1 a moonlit keep and its countryside, realm 2 a wild forest), the 40/60 aim, villages with room,
nothing tall on the camera's side, changes plainly visible from the game camera with before/after shots.

## Checks

Before/after shots of every place changed; the realms' reach, spawns, normals and economy checks; performance (draw
calls) no worse than now on desktop and phones.
