# 094 Whisperwood's set pieces

Group 94 of [the plan for realms 1 and 2](../plans/realms-1-2-revisit.md) (task [081](../in-progress/081-realms-1-2-as-beautiful-as-realm-3.md)).

## What

Moonlit glades: 6-8 openings in the canopy with light shafts (the reef's shaft pass above ground) and pale moss. The Great Tree as a landmark: a violet-green pulse of the Warden's sickness in its crown, seen from the village (in `landmarks`); it blossoms after the victory. Rookfall a gorge that reads: a falling curtain of water with spray, ferns and moss on its walls, mist only below the rim. The High Canopy dressed up high (glowing pods, lanterns on the rope walk). The Wardens' Circle, a mossy ruin and broken arch telling the Warden was once a guardian (the prototype's line). The Fallen Giant a hollow to walk through. A beaver pond on the brook. Wisps over the Blackwater toward the Drowned Shrine.
